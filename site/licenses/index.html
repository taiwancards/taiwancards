TaiwanCards is built with gratitude on freely licensed open resources and official Taiwan language data. Each is linked to its source and license.

Open source

CC-CEDICT — CC BY-SA 4.0

Community Chinese–English dictionary — readings, meanings and traditional/simplified pairs.

Make Me a Hanzi — LGPL / Arphic PL

Stroke-order graphics, radical decomposition and character etymology.

Hanzi Writer — MIT

The stroke-by-stroke writing and animation engine used in the writing trainer.

HanziLookupJS — MIT

Handwriting recognition that turns your drawn strokes into a character, fully offline. Its recognition data (mmah.json) is derived from Make Me a Hanzi stroke data and carries that project's LGPL / Arphic PL terms, not the MIT of the engine itself.

Unicode Unihan — Unicode License

The Unicode Han database — canonical readings and character properties.

中華民國教育部（Ministry of Education, R.O.C.）《國語辭典簡編本》 — CC BY-ND 3.0 TW

Audio for characters and words. Recordings by Taiwan's Ministry of Education, data version 20260626. Files are served unmodified; the app only limits playback to the headword.

使用說明 — the official usage notice, retained in full as the license requires

OpenCC — Apache-2.0

The STCharacters table, used to convert simplified characters into traditional ones when you paste in text. Every substitution it makes is listed back to you.

Fonts

All three are self-hosted. Two of them we ship modified, and that is stated below.

Roboto — SIL OFL 1.1

The interface face, used for Latin text and for pinyin. Self-hosted rather than loaded from Google, so no request leaves for a third party when you open a page.

Noto Sans TC — SIL OFL 1.1

The default face for traditional characters. Also self-hosted.

全字庫正楷體 · TW-Kai — SIL OFL 1.1 · 政府資料開放授權條款 v1

Taiwan's own kaishu brush face from the CNS11643 全字庫, published by 數位發展部. This is the face behind the handwriting option, and its 臺 outline is what the site logo is drawn from. We ship a subset of it: the file is reduced to the characters this app actually uses, and the logo embeds outlines taken from it, so the copy here is modified rather than original.

Reference data

Bodies whose published reference material the app builds on: level lists, stroke inventories, character standards, calendar tables and classification scales. These are factual lists rather than creative works, and are credited here rather than licensed. Where a body released its material under a named license, that license is stated.

教育部 · Taiwan MOE

Ministry of Education standard character forms, the 4808 common-character set and character frequency.

SC-TOP · TOCFL

The official TOCFL graded vocabulary lists, level by level.

國家教育研究院 · NAER / COCT / TBCL

National Academy for Educational Research — the TBCL benchmark, COCT corpus and general word-frequency table.

國語注音符號手冊 · 開放部件 (CC BY 4.0)

Zhuyin symbol recordings from the MOE 國語注音符號手冊 open-components bundle, released separately under CC BY 4.0. 2017 © 教育部,國語注音符號手冊-開放部件.

教育部異體字字典 · 國字筆畫併類表

The normative list of stroke names behind the basic and compound stroke inventory.

教育部《成語典》 (CC BY-ND 3.0 TW)

The official dictionary of 成語: definition, classical source, origin story and examples for every idiom. The Chinese text is shown verbatim — the license forbids adaptation; the English and Russian glosses and notes are written by us.

中央氣象署 · Central Weather Administration

Rainfall scales, typhoon categories and the UV index bands.

茶及飲料作物改良場 · TBRS

Tea classification, processing stages and oxidation levels for Taiwanese cultivars.

香港天文台 · Hong Kong Observatory

The lunar calendar and solar term tables the festival dates are computed from.

小學堂 · Academia Sinica (CC0 1.0)

Traced ancient character forms — oracle bone, bronze and seal script. Released into the public domain.
